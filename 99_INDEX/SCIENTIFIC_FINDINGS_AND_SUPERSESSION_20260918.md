## 4. Human–assistant reuse and dynamics

`RUN-001` embeds the separate Z2 method and outputs. From 33,500 user–assistant–user chains, the method counted 1,958 next-turn adoptions among 44,494 predefined lexical-structural candidates (**4.4006%**) against its shuffle null mean **0.9259%** (empirical p ~0.00020). Reverse-direction adoption is **4.6876%** (2,092/44,628), larger descriptively than the forward rate. This supports temporal structural lexical reuse above this particular shuffle baseline; it does **not** establish preferential assistant→human transfer, unique invention, correct graph-semantic transfer, model-weight effects, or an externally validated causal communication mechanism. Its lexicon is explicitly frozen in `RUN-001/.../RSOS_Z2_RESULTS/Z2_METHOD.json`. The original Z2 summary uses **72,550 visible messages**, whereas WF1-P0 uses **72,639 extracted messages**; different extraction versions and definitions are retained rather than falsely reconciled to one denominator.

WF1-P0 covers 1,775 conversations and analyzes fixed eight-/twelve-message windows using the previously frozen historical F1 labels. The internal original 376-vs-1,399 discovery comparison had severe length differences (~140.1 vs ~14.3 messages); fixed windows reduce but cannot remove all author/topic/lexicon dependencies. Entropy, state diversity and dwell merit prospective testing; negative recurrence and lagged-gain measurements preclude claims of established recurrence or autonomous historical attractor recovery. Chronological adjacency is not a verified parent-child conversation-edge audit.

## 5. Graph intervention causality and representation boundary

V12 and corrective WF1-A manipulate **supplied graph-task prompts** and measure responses. They support causal effects **of those prompt interventions on the measured black-box outputs** under the frozen tasks/controls when their construction is valid. This is not causal identification of a spontaneous RSOS mechanism in the model, training data, weights, hidden circuits or arbitrary external user conversations. The hard-control AUC is discriminating constructed, labeled conditions, not an estimate of architectural rarity. Separately report direction and order effects, individual E8 reversal failure, switch-reassignment diagnostics, preservation and terminal failures, and native aggregate-vs-individual control differences.

F1/F2-B/WF1-B instead score structural/lexical features extracted **passively** from historical/external conversations. The frozen F2-B detector never consumed the experimental V11/V12/WF1-A edge-response measurements; WF1-B reused the passive detector rather than prospectively validating a frozen projection of the ten-edge causal signature. **The invariant connecting historical lineage, active graph intervention, and population rarity has NOT BEEN ESTABLISHED.** WF3 must inventory whether a passively computable representation can be defined without using input-derived or response-label-derived shortcuts. Development and truly fresh confirmation data must be separated.
