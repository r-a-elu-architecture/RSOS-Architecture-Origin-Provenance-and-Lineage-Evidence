WF1-B EXTERNAL POPULATION BENCHMARK — PREREGISTRATION

GOAL
====
Quantify how rare the validated post-WF1-A structural fingerprint is
within an independent external conversation population.

PRIMARY QUESTION
================
How frequently do external conversations fall inside the frozen
RSOS/WF1 structural-signature region?

HARD RULES
==========
1. WF1-A source signature is frozen and cannot be changed after external data inspection.
2. Use only features computable identically from RSOS reference data and external conversations.
3. Blind RSOS names, glyphs, system names, explicit terminology and authorship markers.
4. Match/control conversation length, turn count and observable complexity.
5. Thresholds/classifier definitions are frozen before external scoring.
6. Preserve every external near-match and match.
7. No post-hoc removal of inconvenient external examples.
8. Report:
   - number scored
   - number crossing frozen threshold
   - empirical frequency
   - confidence interval
   - nearest external matches
   - RSOS vs external distribution
9. Do not claim global uniqueness from a finite population.
10. Negative results remain valid results.
